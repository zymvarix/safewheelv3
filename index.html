Why SafeWheel

The rise of unregistered “transporters” on campuses has created safety, reliability, and accountability issues. SafeWheel replaces informal channels with a formal, app‑based system where drivers are verified, trips are tracked, and help is one tap away.

Vision

To become Malaysia’s most trusted and safest student transportation platform, connecting every IPTA with reliable and secure mobility.

Mission

  • Develop high‑quality mobile apps with seamless booking and payments.
  • Make transportation easily accessible across IPTA campuses.
  • Provide safe, affordable, and reliable service through verified drivers and efficient operations.

Objectives (Year 1)

  • 100+ registered drivers and multi‑campus rollout.
  • ≥90% user satisfaction; average wait under 10 minutes.
  • 2,500+ registered student users.

Pricing

Rider fare: Value‑based pricing around RM0.85 per minute (about 15% below a RM1.00/min benchmark). An average 6–8 minute campus ride is roughly RM5.10–RM6.80.

Driver commission: 10% platform commission per completed ride to encourage student participation and service availability.

How it works (Riders)

  1. Open the SafeWheel app and set pickup and destination within campus.
  2. Get matched with the nearest verified driver and track live arrival.
  3. Ride, pay in‑app via e‑wallet/online banking, and rate your trip.

Operating hours

Service typically runs 7:00 AM – 10:00 PM on academic days, with peak coverage during class start, lunch, and evening periods.

Driver registration

  1. Submit IC, driver’s license, insurance, and road tax.
  2. Provide background check (external drivers) or university ID (affiliated drivers).
  3. Attend orientation on policies and app usage.
  4. Maintain strong ratings; low ratings may lead to suspension.
